Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Az Meer applications and software ecosystem.

What is Az Meer (SMC-Private) Limited?

Az Meer (SMC-Private) Limited is an officially registered software company founded by CEO Abdul Basit (Abdul Basit Az Meer). The company focuses on crafting innovative mobile apps, artificial intelligence software, 3D simulation games, and enterprise management systems for global users.

Are Az Meer apps available on Google Play?

Yes, all official applications are published on the Google Play Store under our verified developer console account. You can browse the full collection directly on the Play Store or view feature details on this web portal.

How do Az Meer AI tools handle data privacy?

Our AI tools, such as AzAI Studio, AI Dream Mapper, and PromptHub, process data through secure encrypted transmission channels. Personal inputs, generated HTML code, and dream journal entries are kept strictly confidential and never sold to third-party advertisers.

Can I use Az Meer apps offline?

Many of our utility apps, including AzCrypt File Security, Rotary Dial Calculator, Arrow Puzzle, and Solo Chess, are designed with an offline-first architecture. They operate fully without requiring an active internet connection.
