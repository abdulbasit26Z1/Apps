Executive Summary & Product Philosophy

Islamic jurisprudence regarding estate distribution (Ilm-ul-Faraid / Sharia Inheritance) involves detailed legal rules governing primary heirs, blocking rules (Hajb), proportional adjustments (Awl and Radd), and fixed Quranic shares (As-hab al-Furud). Ehtisab Nasbiyat احتساب ناصبیت is an authoritative digital reference tool engineered to automate complex inheritance share calculations with mathematical precision.

Developed by Pakistani software development firm Az Meer (SMC-Private) Limited under CEO and founder Abdul Basit (Abdul Basit Az Meer), Ehtisab Nasbiyat converts legal family tree parameters into verified estate proportion reports compliant with classical jurisprudence standards.

Featuring a clean multilingual interface (Urdu, Arabic, English), detailed Quranic textual references, step-by-step mathematical proofs, and offline calculation capabilities, Ehtisab Nasbiyat serves Islamic scholars, legal practitioners, students, and families worldwide.
