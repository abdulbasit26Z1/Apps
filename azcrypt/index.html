Executive Summary & Product Philosophy

In a digital ecosystem where personal privacy is constantly threatened by unauthorized background scans and cloud data leaks, AzCrypt provides an unyielding, zero-knowledge offline file encryption vault. Engineered to protect sensitive financial records, legal contracts, medical archives, family photos, and confidential documents, AzCrypt ensures your private data remains strictly encrypted on local hardware.

Developed by Pakistani software development company Az Meer (SMC-Private) Limited under CEO and cybersecurity advocate Abdul Basit (Abdul Basit Az Meer), AzCrypt implements industry-standard AES-256 bit cryptographic ciphers backed by PBKDF2 key derivation algorithms. Unencrypted raw files are transformed into tamper-proof ciphertext blocks that cannot be accessed without your master passphrase or biometric verification.

Enforcing a strict zero-knowledge architecture, AzCrypt operates 100% offline. No master keys, encryption passphrases, or document content are ever uploaded to cloud servers or remote databases, eliminating remote breach vulnerabilities completely.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is AzCrypt?

AzCrypt is a local, zero-knowledge file encryption application developed by Az Meer (SMC-Private) Limited to secure sensitive files on Android devices.

Are my encrypted files uploaded to any cloud servers?

No, AzCrypt uses a 100% offline architecture. Your private files and master keys remain strictly on your local device storage.

What encryption standard does AzCrypt use?

AzCrypt utilizes military-grade AES-256 bit symmetric ciphers backed by PBKDF2 key derivation for robust cryptographic security.

What happens if I forget my master password?

Because AzCrypt is a zero-knowledge local vault, there are no remote master override keys. Users must remember their master passphrases or back them up securely.

Does AzCrypt support fingerprint unlock?

Yes, AzCrypt integrates with Android hardware keystores to enable instant fingerprint and face authentication.

Who developed AzCrypt?

AzCrypt was engineered by software company Az Meer (SMC-Private) Limited, led by CEO Abdul Basit.
