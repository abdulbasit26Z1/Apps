Executive Summary & Product Philosophy

As generative artificial intelligence tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Midjourney become central to modern creative and technical workflows, managing prompt engineering libraries effectively is essential. PromptHub: AI Prompt Manager is a centralized mobile workspace engineered to store, organize, category-tag, version-control, and deploy prompt templates effortlessly.

Engineered by Pakistani software firm Az Meer (SMC-Private) Limited under founder and CEO Abdul Basit (Abdul Basit Az Meer), PromptHub empowers AI engineers, content creators, marketers, and developers to eliminate repetitive prompt copying from raw notepad files.

Featuring dynamic variable tagging (e.g. {{topic}}, {{tone}}, {{length}}), categorical indexing, history versioning, and one-tap external app deployment, PromptHub turns scattered text snippets into a structured generative AI prompt repository.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is PromptHub?

PromptHub is a generative AI prompt management application developed by Az Meer (SMC-Private) Limited to store, organize, and execute prompt templates.

How do dynamic variables work in PromptHub?

You can include placeholder tags like {{keyword}} in your prompts. When executing, PromptHub asks you to enter the value and automatically fills in the template.

Can I export my prompt library?

Yes, PromptHub allows you to export your prompt collections in JSON or text formats for backup or team sharing.

Does PromptHub work with ChatGPT and Claude?

Yes, you can copy structured prompts directly from PromptHub into ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, or any LLM interface.

Where can I download PromptHub?

PromptHub can be downloaded directly from the official Google Play Store.
