Executive Summary & Product Philosophy

Tic Tac Toe is one of humanity’s most enduring strategy board games. Az XoXo: Tic Tac Toe AI & 2P modernizes this timeless puzzle experience by combining adaptive single-player AI difficulty engines with fluid offline two-player pass-and-play multiplayer capabilities.

Developed by Pakistani software development firm Az Meer (SMC-Private) Limited under founder and CEO Abdul Basit (Abdul Basit Az Meer), Az XoXo replaces simple pen-and-paper grids with glowing neon themes, responsive haptic controls, and match analytics.

Whether challenging intelligent AI algorithms on Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty levels, or competing face-to-face with friends on a shared screen, Az XoXo delivers quick, engaging logic fun suitable for all ages.
