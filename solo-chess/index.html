Executive Summary & Product Philosophy

Chess mastery requires disciplined tactical training, pattern recognition, and endgame calculation. Solo Chess by Az Meer is a specialized single-player tactical chess training application engineered to sharpen board vision through curated puzzle drills, checkmate challenges, and endgame scenarios.

Developed by Pakistani software company Az Meer (SMC-Private) Limited under founder and CEO Abdul Basit (Abdul Basit Az Meer), Solo Chess removes matchmaking wait times by focusing entirely on self-paced single-player tactical problem solving.

Featuring Mate-in-1, Mate-in-2, and Mate-in-3 puzzle tiers, custom high-contrast piece themes, offline puzzle databases, and move analysis feedback, Solo Chess offers the ultimate training tool for novice players and tournament contenders alike.
