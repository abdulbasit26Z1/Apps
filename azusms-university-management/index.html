Executive Summary & Product Philosophy

Modern higher education institutions require unified digital administrative platforms to coordinate academic schedules, student enrollments, faculty gradebooks, financial tuition fees, and campus communications. Az USMS: University Management System is a comprehensive mobile administrative suite engineered to centralize campus operations into a secure role-based portal.

Engineered by Pakistani software firm Az Meer (SMC-Private) Limited under founder and CEO Abdul Basit (Abdul Basit Az Meer), Az USMS caters to university administrators, academic faculty members, enrolled students, and campus accounting departments through intuitive mobile dashboards.

Featuring real-time attendance logging, automated GPA calculations, course schedule management, fee invoice tracking, and encrypted cloud synchronization, Az USMS reduces administrative overhead while empowering students with instant mobile access to academic records.
