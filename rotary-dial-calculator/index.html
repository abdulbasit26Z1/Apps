Executive Summary & Product Philosophy

Standard touch-screen keypad calculators are often sterile and prone to accidental key mis-taps. Rotary Dial Calculator re-imagines everyday arithmetic by introducing an interactive rotary wheel that turns numerical input into a satisfying tactile interaction reminiscent of vintage rotary telephones.

Engineered by Pakistani software firm Az Meer (SMC-Private) Limited under CEO and founder Abdul Basit (Abdul Basit Az Meer), Rotary Dial Calculator merges vintage aesthetics with modern mathematical precision, haptic vibration feedback, and dual-ring scientific capabilities.

With over 50,000+ active installs on Google Play, Rotary Dial Calculator features a historical calculation tape memory, customizable Bakelite and chrome visual skins, zero battery drain, and 100% offline functionality.
